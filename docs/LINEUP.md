# Lineup

The long-term goal is one Albion engine and a world pack for every Fable the studio head can legally point at. The rewrites are useful on their own. The merge is the point.

Primary focus is Fable 2 GOTY, then Fable 3. Fable 3 work starts when the Fable 2 rewrite is 1:1, as defined in `docs/GOTY.md`. Not before.

| World | Role | Starts |
|---|---|---|
| Fable 2 GOTY | First rewrite. Builds the engine. | Now |
| Fable 3 | Second rewrite on that engine. Primary after Fable 2. | The day Fable 2 is 1:1 |
| Fable | Original. Study cards only until the spine walks. | After Fable 3 is playable |
| Fable: The Lost Chapters | Original plus Northern Wastes. Same era as Fable, not a Fable 2 decoder job. | With Fable |
| Fable Anniversary | Same quests as TLC, later presentation. A presentation pack, not a third story. | With TLC |
| Fable Legends | Twelve beta tales. No complete retail game. A pack only if a legal source exists. | Last |

Knothole Island and See the Future are Fable 2 GOTY content. Traitor's Keep and Understone are Fable 3 content. They are not separate engines.

A rewrite that cannot load the next world is not the engine. A world that forks the runtime is not a pack.
